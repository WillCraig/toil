The life of a card payment at Overdraft Federal, from the shopper's click to the merchant's bank account.

  1. Authorize and capture. Checkout sends the card (or a stored token) to the payments service, which calls the processor. Every capture carries an idempotency key; a retry with the same key must never capture twice (ADR-007).
  2. Settle. Overnight, Settlement batches captured payments per merchant into settlements, cut at the merchant's local midnight (ADO #176 fixes a cut that ran a day early for merchants on Eastern time).
  3. Reserve. High-risk merchants keep a rolling reserve: a share of each settlement held back for a fixed number of days (ADR-011, GitHub #18).
  4. Pay out. Settlements roll up into payouts on the merchant's schedule, daily or weekly, sent by ACH.
  5. Refund and dispute. Refunds come off the next payout. Disputes (chargebacks) hold the disputed amount until we win or lose them.
  6. Reconcile. At 02:00 UTC the reconciliation job checks the ledger against the processor's settlement file.
