Status: Accepted, September 30. Owner: Mei.

Decision

  • Merchants choose daily or weekly payouts. Weekly payouts go out on Fridays by ACH.
  • High-risk merchants keep a rolling reserve of 10% of each settlement for 14 days (GitHub #18).
  • Merchants see the next payout before it happens: the Payout preview (GitHub #17, Figma "Overdraft Federal: Payout preview").

Consequences

Schedule changes need care (see A merchant changed payout schedule). The preview waits on a shared idempotency store (#12), so a preview can never show a payment twice.
