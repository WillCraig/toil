  • Store and send money as whole cents (amount_minor), never as floating-point dollars: $12.50 is 1250.
  • Fees and interest are worked out per transaction in fractions of a cent and rounded once, on the statement, half up. Rounding each transaction down is what left refunds $0.01 short (view payments.refund_shortfalls).
  • A refund returns exactly the cents that were captured, never a recalculated amount.
  • Format for people as -$1,234.50: dollar sign, thousands separators, two decimals, minus sign first. formatAmount in src/money.js prints -12.35 USD today (GitHub #38).
