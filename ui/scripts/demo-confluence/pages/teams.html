Where data lives

  • Read replica (Postgres, schema payments): payments, refunds, disputes, payouts, job_runs. Read-only for everyone; nobody writes to it by hand.
  • Finance ledger (SQLite, the old one): merchants, settlements, chargebacks. Finance still reconciles against it monthly.
  • Designs: Figma. Release checklist and this knowledge base: Confluence.

On-call

One primary per week, rotating Platform, Settlement, Checkout. The primary owns the nightly jobs (reconciliation at 02:00 UTC, statement export at 01:55 UTC) and the dispute webhook.
