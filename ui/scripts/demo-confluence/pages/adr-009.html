Status: Accepted, October 20. Owner: Priya.

Context

Stored cards (save at checkout, charge later) need somewhere to keep card data. Keeping PANs in the payments service puts all of it in PCI scope.

Decision

  • Card numbers are exchanged for tokens at the edge, before they reach the payments service.
  • The vault stores tokens and their encrypted cards, and nothing else touches a PAN.
  • Vault keys rotate quarterly without re-tokenizing (GitHub #8).

Consequences

This is the critical path for stored cards. Jira SCRUM-14 (store tokens in the vault) blocks SCRUM-17 (tokenize at the edge), which blocks SCRUM-18, SCRUM-21 and SCRUM-30; SCRUM-14 also blocks SCRUM-15 (charge a stored token). The same work: ADO #152 → #169, GitHub #7 → #8.
