Severity: 2. Duration: 9 hours. Facilitator: Mei.

What happened

Settlement batches for merchants on Eastern time were cut at UTC midnight instead of the merchant's own, so a day's late-evening payments were settled and paid out a day early. Northwind Coffee's finance team noticed the totals didn't match their own report.

Why

The batch job converts the cut-off to UTC once, at the start of the run, using the server's zone. A daylight-saving change that weekend moved it by an hour, and the merchant's zone was never consulted.
