How you know

A shopper or merchant reports two charges about 30 seconds apart. In the replica, two captured rows in payments.payments share one idempotency_key. It happens roughly once a day, nearly always after a double click or a client retry on a slow 3-D Secure challenge.

What to do now

  1. Find both rows by idempotency key and confirm the amounts and merchant match.
  2. Refund the later capture in full. Use reason duplicate (see Refund reason codes).
  3. Reply to the merchant with the refund reference. Do not promise a timeline for the fix.

Why it happens

The service records the idempotency key after the processor call returns. Two requests that arrive inside that window both call the processor. ADR-007 moves the check before the call; PR #37 implements it; GitHub #12 makes the key store shared across instances.
