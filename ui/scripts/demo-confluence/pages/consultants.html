Deliverables Unlimited's consultants work inside our tools with their own accounts: Jira, Azure DevOps, GitHub, Confluence and read-only access to the payments replica. They don't get a copy of anything; they connect to ours.

Access

  • Each consultant gets an Overdraft Federal account per tool, requested by Sam Rivera and approved by the team lead who owns the tool.
  • Tokens are personal, scoped to what the consultant works on, and expire with the statement of work.
  • The payments replica is read-only for everyone. Nobody connects to production.
