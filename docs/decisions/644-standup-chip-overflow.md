# Stand-up team chips cut inside their box (#644)

- **The team line's chips are a declared truncation, not a wrap.** They now carry `data-truncate`, like the per-person chips beside them already did. The title stays on one line with an ellipsis, and the whole sentence (every fact the chip folds, with its time) stays in the chip's tooltip (`chipTitle`). Wrapping would make one chip two or three lines tall in a row of one-line chips, and the per-person rows already cut the same way. `ui/src/views/Standup.tsx`, `TeamLine`.
- **The box does the cutting, not a character count.** The team chip used to cut its text to 40 characters in code before the CSS saw it. That dropped "by <name>" or the destination sprint even at 1440px, with room to spare. It now renders the whole fact and lets the chip's width cut it. The per-person chips keep their `TITLE_CHARS` cut, because this change doesn't touch them.
- **The layout check opens a day whose sprint moves are long, rather than the sample gaining one.** `#/project/settlement/standup/2026-10-05` is the sample sprint's first day. Its team line comes from the fixture items' own history ("moved from Payments\Sprint 41 to Payments\Sprint 42, by K. Nakamura"), and without the fix that chip overflows at 375px. An earlier draft scripted a sprint move into the sample worklog as an observed event. Review found that no real sync can produce that kind of event, and that it contradicted the sample's sprints, so it was dropped. `ui/scripts/layout-check.mjs`, `ROUTES`; a unit test in `ui/test/standup-views.test.tsx` pins the attribute and the tooltip on the same chip.
- **Touch reachability of a declared truncation is left to #649** (the maintainer's call, "Ship, file issue"). The principle review found that a `title` tooltip never shows on a touch screen, so a cut chip's end is reachable only one step away. Every stand-up chip already followed this pattern, so this change matches it, and the rule for what a `data-truncate` keeps reachable without hover is decided there.
